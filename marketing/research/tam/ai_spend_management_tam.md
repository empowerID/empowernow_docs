- Overall AI market: $294B (2025) → $1.77T (2032) at 29.2% CAGR; other estimate $391B (2025) → $3.50T (2033) at 31.5% CAGR. Bain: AI HW/SW $780–$990B by 2027 at 40–55% CAGR.
- Adjacent markets: Cloud Cost Mgmt $9.8B (2024, 17.2% CAGR 2025–2034); AI Workload Mgmt $33.5B (2024) → $478.8B (2033) at 34.4% CAGR; AI Observability to $10.7B by 2033 at 22.5% CAGR.

### AI Spend Management TAM Estimate (2030)
- Conservative: $5–$10B
- Aggressive: $15–$25B

Rationale: Surging AI adoption and spend volatility (token/GPU), intersection of cloud cost mgmt, AI observability, and workload orchestration; average AI app spend ~$400k with ~75% YoY growth; need for runtime guardrails and governance.

### Growth Drivers
- Explosive AI spend and CFO/FinOps pressure for guardrails and chargeback
- Runtime governance needs: budgets, 402 semantics, streaming caps, receipts
- Regulatory/compliance momentum and enterprise standardization

### Market Evolution
- 2025–2027: Rapid expansion, early best practices and reference architectures
- 2027–2030: Consolidation, standards (e.g., receipts, plan contracts), platformization
- 2030+: Mature category; integrated governance + observability + cost control
